What's inside

Everything a hive visit needs, in one place

No spreadsheets, no scribbled notes that fade in the rain. HiveDash keeps each colony's story together so the next visit picks up where the last one left off.

Step-by-step inspections

Walk through queen, brood, stores and temperament in order, and finish with a clear health read for the colony.

Voice notes & photos

Hands full of frames? Talk it through or snap a photo — both attach straight to the inspection record.

Health & honey trends

Watch colony strength, honey yield and hive temperature shift week to week, so problems show up early.

Tasks & reminders

Treatments, feeding, re-checks — HiveDash flags what's due and what's overdue, per hive.

Photo insights

Snap a frame and let HiveDash help read what it's looking at — brood pattern, stores and more.

Offline-first, then synced

Everything works with zero signal at the hives. When you reconnect, your records sync to every device.
